WEIGH YOURSELF
The scale fluctuates daily based on water, sodium, carbs, and sleep — daily readings mean nothing on their own. What matters is your 7-day rolling average. Weigh yourself every morning and look at the weekly trend, not the daily number.
PROTEIN
Protein is non-negotiable. On days where calories slip, carbs and fat can flex — protein cannot. Hitting your protein target is the single most important nutrition variable for building muscle.
INTENSITY
Take every set to 0–3 reps in the tank. Being as close to failure as possible is what signals to your body that it NEEDS that muscle and is what drives it to grow.
STEPS
Hit 8,000 steps every day as a general activity floor. This keeps you healthy and active without digging into recovery. Your separate cardio sessions are on top of this.
SLEEP
Aim for 7–9 hours every night. Recovery happens outside the gym — sleep is where muscle is built. Poor sleep raises cortisol, increases hunger, and tanks performance. Treat it like training.
MISSED SESSIONS
If you miss a session, don't try to cram it in or double up. Just pick up where you left off on the next day. Feel free to move sessions around your week as needed — the structure matters, the specific days don't. One missed session changes nothing — consistency over weeks and months is what matters.
PROGRESSION RULES
1
Track every session
Log weight and reps for every working set. Be mindful of how you feel.
2
Add reps first
When you hit the top of the rep range for all sets, add reps next session before adding weight.
3
Then add weight
Once you consistently hit the top of the rep range, add 2.5–5lbs and reset to the bottom of the rep range.
4
Stall protocol
If strength stalls for 2+ consecutive weeks, drop weight 10% and rebuild. Don't grind the same numbers indefinitely.
5
Deload every 6–8 weeks
Reduce volume by ~40–50% and keep the same exercises and intensity. Performance rebound the following week is normal and expected.

THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND

Energy, mood and sleep
How did you feel day to day this week? Were you tired, flat, or did you feel good going into sessions?
Strength and performance feel
Did you feel stronger or weaker than last week? Were sessions hard, comfortable, or did something feel off?
Nutrition adherence
Was hitting your targets easy or a struggle this week? Any days that went significantly off track?
Pain or discomfort
Anything unusual around your hips, back or joints during or after training? Flag anything early — don't push through pain
